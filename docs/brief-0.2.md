# v0.2 — one novel.global

Three builds become one experience. **A**, the aura at `/` (`src/base/aura.js`), stays the way in: the smooth, quiet journey (notice, W.I.S.H., art, print). **C**, the studio at `/studio`, is the deep engine: the map, the devices, the lives, the sound. **B**, the base at `/?base` (`src/base/base.js`), stops being a site of its own: its net, its 4-track, its pixel board and its packs move into C as devices.

The rule for every stage: A always stays one touch away and is always where one returns. Nothing is layered on top of A that it did not ask for. What is deep is reached through one object at a time, never a menu.

Two worlds, kept apart on purpose: the pixel, the digital and the analogue (C's devices, B's pixels, LEDs, OLEDs) against the natural, the ethereal and the mythical (A's irukandji, blueprint, chalk and stars). They sit side by side and answer each other; they are never blended.

## Stages, in order (one at a time, each checked on the preview)

### Stage 1 — the journey, smoothed (A only)
1. **The globe's rings:** NOTICE is the irukandji's eye with its tears. It blinks and its pupil pulses softly. The same icon is the notice tab. W.I.S.H. stays the jellyfish, PRINT stays the receipt, all three drawn in one style.
2. **Out of order:** W.I.S.H., art and print open from the globe at any time. Art with nothing begun offers a blank receipt to start on: a hero photograph and words, made in seconds, for quick prints and zines.
3. **Calm type:** each step's line sits centred in the middle; the dotted lines one writes on are half as heavy and fainter.
4. **One word first:** opening a step flashes one plain word on each key or field ("intention", "photo", "words", "print"…), one after another, quick and readable, then gone; the longer lines follow as before.
5. **The intention:** the paper icon sits a little lower and the words typed follow it.
6. **Art:** the hero photograph first in the tray, then paste, then words; the W.I.S.H. lines can be moved below the picture.
7. **Print and the wall:** the close on a poster small; each poster shows plainly where it stands (on the wall only, waiting, printed); a check before anything is started again or taken down; nothing blank or unfinished hangs on the wall. The tentacle lets go of the print key on the print page and becomes the line the receipts are clipped to; every receipt sits on it whatever its length.
8. **Print, three ways:** to the wall only, to the 80 mm printer, or to the printer and the mesh.
9. **Small links:** a life's card has a key to its place on the map and back; El Niño a key to more.

### Stage 2 — words from the approval page
Every heading, line and hover on INTENTION and W.I.S.H. rewritable on the approval page (TRACKS), as BOARD 01's template is. Empty goes back to the site's own words.

### Stage 3 — the intel ring
The object under the eye (the catch) opens into the intel: the map, the lives, the data, El Niño, as now, and from there into C's devices, each opened in place with a way straight back to A. Each poster made earns one new object (at most three) on a slow horizontal ring under the eye: a device of C brought into A (the radio, the board, the lives), so depth arrives as it is earned and never as clutter.

### Stage 4 — sound as an instrument
The sound key (top right) opens, with an irukandji of its own, one dashboard for everything musical: B's 4-track and the web's voice, C's layers and moods, the radar's sweep, constellations and journeys as songs. Compositions saved, played while exploring, downloaded as 45 second loops, sent as a link or a QR poster.

### Stage 5 — places
Search finds places and pins on the map and the map finds them in search. New cities, data packs and custom builds from the approval page.

## What stays
The engine, the receipts, the printers, the touch screen and the mesh, the studio's links and every code ever printed.
