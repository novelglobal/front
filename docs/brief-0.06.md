## The idea in one line

The story begins with heat. Someone arrives in the hottest month ahead, watches time wind back to today, and is asked one
question: **what have you noticed?** From there the site opens one piece at a time, in the order of the praxis poem
(NOTICED, RESPONSE, DIRECT ACTION). Each piece appears only when it is needed, is answered with a sound and a light (never an
arrow or a sentence), and the journey ends with a receipt: printed now, or held until the printer is back.

## What changes in the pages

- **RADIO.** The quick radio and the small W.I.S.H. intention device become one rig: the intention device is a module
  plugged into the radio with an RS-232 to USB-C cable (beige DB9 plug, coiled lead, a USB-C at the radio's side). Its
  receipt shows small over the map (pressed, close up); its examples key stays with it. The studio side (turned over) is
  unchanged. On a phone the module docks as a slim strip on top of the radio, so the rig stays short enough to see the map.
- **W.I.S.H. MAKER.** One page, no quick side: the full W.I.S.H. device as it is now, the board, the book. The flip key goes.
  SELECT stays, for the board.
- **The first board's template.** A W.I.S.H. matrix: four areas, W, I, S, H, each a section with a short note saying what
  research to paste there. Around them, small notes in the manner of a game's codec or field manual (Kojima, Metal Gear),
  explaining each tool of the board in a few plain words: NOTE, SECT, LINK, IMG, ZOOM, LOCK, OUT, and how the board is used
  (strategic creative mapping, explained to a five-year-old). Every piece can be deleted. Its words can be edited from the
  approval page, the same way BOARD 00 is edited now.
- **PRINT.**
  - The quick side is the 80 mm receipt with the same tools as the studio side (words, stickers, tape, the photo looks),
    and a choice of printer when more than one is online, each with its light and its place.
  - The studio side is for the other outputs only: PNG, ESC/POS, the OLED screen, mesh, other paper sizes, what is waiting,
    and where every printer is.
- **The W.I.S.H. devices stay as they are.**

## Sound: deep ambient and experimental dub, at 120 BPM

What is wrong now: high whistling and music-box voices (the whine), the horn and driven filters (the honk), glass chimes on
every reward (the dings), and notes at any pitch at any moment.

The fix:
- **One key, one tempo.** Every cell's note is moved to the nearest note of one low minor scale, and every sound lands on a
  sixteenth of a 120 BPM bar, always (not only in El Niño mode).
- **Fewer, deeper voices.** LIFE a dub chord stab into tape echo; WATER a sub note that drops; COMMUNITY filtered dust and
  noise; POLLUTION a low detuned stab. The whistle, music box, horn and glass bells leave the moods; still no drums.
- **Never a wall.** At most two sounds a sixteenth, and a soft low-pass on the whole mix, so it stays deep, not shrill.
- **Rewards in the same key.** Each gate adds one note to a rising figure (one, two, three, four, then the full chord), made
  from the dub stab, not glass.
- **El Niño.** With everything at 120 BPM, the switch changes heat, not speed: the filters open, the sound grows harder,
  and the radar runs red.

## Sense check: what to watch

1. **Real data only.** The opening shows heat risk, not deaths. There is no honest forecast of deaths by species; there is
   the Bureau's outlook and each life's sensitivity to heat (`degOf`). The patches and fading marks show that risk, true to
   the rule: the information is real, the presentation can be fantasy.
2. **Sound must wait for a tap.** Browsers forbid sound before a first touch, so the opening is silent and the first sound is
   the reward for answering the question. That is a better beat anyway.
3. **Never stuck.**
   - Every gate can be passed in one clear action, and the next target always signals itself.
   - Print works offline: pressing it completes the journey and the receipt waits in the queue.
   - With motion off (Settings, or the phone's setting), the opening is a still frame and every hint is a steady glow.
4. **The tests.** All 231 checks assume every tab is visible. They will start with the journey already passed, and one new
   check will walk every gate, the offline print and the easter egg.
5. **Phones.** The radio with its plugged-in module must fit on the rail with the map in view; the module docks slim.
6. **Scope.** This is four or five sessions of work. Each stage below ships on its own and is useful alone.
