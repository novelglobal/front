## Codes and access

Each family of code has its own shape and its own owner, so a code from one never works as another.

| Code | Looks like | Belongs to | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| W.I.S.H. piece | `DA-XXXX` | I W.I.S.H. | That piece: its talk, its reprint, its place on the map |
| Maker's code ("Your code") | A word and a mark | I W.I.S.H. | That person's pieces on any phone |
| Pairing code | 8 letters, lasting a quarter of an hour | The engine | An island joining |
| Keeper's key | 32 characters, on the welcome slip | The engine, one island | That island's control panel, never another |
| Island find-code | Island number and 4 letters, e.g. `2·K7QF` | That island | Its card on this phone | H2 |
| Song | `#songs=…` | RADIO | That song |

## Connections

A printer switches on its connections. Only then does that kind of thing work on it, or its own get shared with the islands on the same connection.

| Connection | Switched on, it lets |
|---|---|
| W.I.S.H. | W.I.S.H. pieces print here (enforced by the site: a piece to an island without it is refused) |
| RADIO | Songs play and print here |
| ART | Styles, stamps and icons are shared both ways |
| WORDS | Copy (headlines, lines) is shared both ways |
| CARDS | Its experiences show as cards and can be wired on the switchboard |
| CODES | Its find-codes open the other islands on CODES |

- **novel.global's printer and the starting ones** (every printer paired before 1.1, or paired from the approval page) have every connection on, and two-way. This allows true remixing and testing.
- **A self-joined island** starts with none. Its keeper switches them on in YOUR ISLAND, and novel.global can change them on the approval page (CONNECTIONS).
- **An island's own stacks** always print on its own printer.
