# Brief 0.07: the master plan's next builds

The owner's list of 22 October, and what was built for each.

1. **Colour outside PRINT.** Posters, cards, the receipt over the map and on the board: the life's photograph in colour at its largest, with its common name. PRINT's strip and what prints stay in ink.
2. **The showcase as a carousel of pairs.** Each poster with its card beside it, the card at a tarot's length; the close-up key opens the pair full screen on any screen, zoomed in place, never a new window.
3. **Songs from links.** A QR, a code or a mix opened arms a song key; the first touch plays that place's song round and round in the background, without leaving the page; a small key keeps it among the loops.
4. **Only lives make receipts.** A sighting or a record of one's own; places are tied to the life held or only named. Places are drawn quiet until their tag is up.
5. **More life and the ground.** A year of mammals apart, six years of the same weeks; water, parks, gardens and green spaces always shown as patches, opened by a double press.
6. **The clip pinned.** The receipt rolls up and drops from where its head hung.
7. **PRINT on a phone.** The strip opens wide; a zoom key on the dongle.
8. **The dongle's keys.** A photograph key where Aa was; words typed on the receipt; the yellow tie the right way round.
9. **One jellyfish, slow,** at the unlock.
10. **Small things.** RADIO's middle screw gone; the equaliser moves with the music and pulses the extreme months, every month in colour; El Niño turns the radar faster; the board opens further out, ZOOM second; the instructions shortened; 1.5 ft the longest strip.

Also, from the owner's notes the same day: SELECT never open without its wire (it moves to the device in front, or closes); the example goes onto the receipt already there; the sticker packs scroll on a desk; the card's keys read to the W.I.S.H. board, then to the print shop.

Left for later: green and water shapes from OpenStreetMap (the patches today are the groups' own ground and the creeks); sampling a song into another, and sharing a song as its own link; the equaliser on RADIO's front as well as its studio side.
