# Brief 0.08: the base build

The owner has it printing at home (the photograph on novel.global is theirs), and asked for this to be the base, most
functional build, with these tweaks:

1. **SELECT carries words and photographs of one's own;** the devices lose those keys. PRINT gets a key to start again.
2. **Day and night:** a sun for DAY, a moon for NIGHT.
3. **Sound after Boadicea (Enya, 1987):** no backing tracks. A choir's hum for clusters, a depth charge for lives in danger, a
   round low note for the rest, a dub echo and a long hall under it all. Three filters (MIST, HALL, DEPTH) take the ding, the
   squeak and the honk out; RADIO's knobs choose them instead of setting volume. The new default on a first visit.
4. **The printer itself on novel.global:** the photograph from above, its lights live, the paper out of its own slot when
   something prints, the words over the lid while it does.

Not tried on a real phone yet: the choir and the depth charge on a phone speaker (they sit low; a phone may lose the depth
charge's lowest part, the thump stays).
